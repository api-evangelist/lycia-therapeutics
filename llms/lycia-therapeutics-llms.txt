# Lycia Therapeutics

> Lycia Therapeutics is a privately held, research-stage biotechnology company in South
> San Francisco, California, developing targeted protein degradation medicines on its
> proprietary lysosomal targeting chimera (LYTAC) platform. LYTAC molecules bind a
> disease-driving extracellular protein and shuttle it into the cell for destruction by
> the lysosome, addressing circulating and membrane-bound proteins that small molecules
> and antibodies have been unable to drug. Lycia publishes no public API, developer
> portal, or machine-readable API artifacts.
